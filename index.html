Attendance Camera

Center your face in the guide and hold still. The scanner checks that one face is visible, sufficiently large, well-lit, and stable before capturing.

Choose Start Camera to grant camera access. No capture button is needed.
  • Face the camera directly and remove anything covering your face.
  • Use even lighting; avoid strong backlighting and shadows.
  • Keep your face inside the oval and hold still until capture.

Keep the attendance tab open. The image is sent directly to the tab that opened this page; this GitHub Pages site does not upload it to a server. This scanner does not perform liveness detection.
